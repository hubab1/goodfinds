The existing branch changes also introduce typed vehicle criteria and mileage cohorts, fresh discovery for each scheduled occurrence, browser preflight/recovery, one-panel handoff and protection against overwriting newer search edits.

On October 25, the 07:59, 09:56 and 11:57 UTC wakes all returned empty batches before their intended local slots. The installed executable still predated this branch's timing fix. The repair therefore also requires installing the validated executable and using its packaged helper while the chat retains an older mounted server. An early wake within five minutes of the next permitted slot waits until that slot and requests a fresh batch UUID; replaying the original empty receipt cannot retry eligibility. Quiet hours remain strict. Repeated missed occurrences must surface as an execution failure rather than routine quiet notifications.

AutoTrader consent remains a browser limitation, not proof of complete coverage. Missing history, unresolved mechanical condition, weak gearbox evidence and town-level journeys remain visible checks. Price comparisons require equivalent current vehicles with known units; seller statements, platform checks and independent verification remain distinct. No seller contact, paid history check or purchase is implied by these improvements.
