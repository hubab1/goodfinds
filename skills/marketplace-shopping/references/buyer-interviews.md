## Details beyond a marketplace's filters

Create a stable field for a stated observable detail, even when it has no marketplace filter. For example, a requested granddad collar can be `collar_style: granddad`; low-heel boots can use `heel_height_cm` with an upper bound. Preserve the buyer's term and importance, form useful keyword queries, then verify the attribute from the listing description, clear images or reliable model specifications. Exact-string matching happens on normalized structured attributes, not arbitrary title text. Expand query wording only to equivalent terms; neighbouring styles are not interchangeable. Unknown or conflicting evidence for a hard requirement stays under review.

Specificity comes from the brief and useful results, rather than from asking every buyer about every possible feature. “Reliable” or “comfortable” is an intent to help shortlist, not an invented verified boolean. Explain an evidence-based proxy before using it as a requirement. User-supplied hard details remain in matching even when no additional questions are necessary. A soft preference remains preferred; uncertain evidence must not masquerade as a match.

## Budget and evidence boundaries

Resolve ambiguous money before mapping a value to `price_minor`. This implementation compares full asking prices. For vehicles, monthly finance, deposits, term, annual mileage, APR and final payment remain separate observed terms; a monthly amount must not be saved as a total cash budget. If the buyer wants finance/lease offers, explain the different basis and collect its terms; do not present a cash-price bargain comparison from those amounts. For clothing or shipped goods, item price, platform fees and delivery are distinct; clarify an all-in budget when requested and record whether the evidence establishes it. Never fill unknown costs with zero.

Buyer interviews collect buyer decisions. Inspection collects seller facts. Ask “Would you consider a repaired write-off?” when relevant, rather than asking the buyer whether an unknown listing has been written off. Preserve the answer's scope and check evidence per candidate.
